SPACE DUEL

1v1 laser dogfight. First to 3 rounds wins.

Controls

PC: click the game to capture the mouse, then aim with the mouse. Space or left click fires. Shift or right click boosts. X brakes. W A S D or arrow keys also steer. I inverts the Y axis, M mutes.

Phone or tablet: left stick steers, FIRE shoots, BOOST speeds up, BRAKE slows down.
